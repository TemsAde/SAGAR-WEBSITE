The Sagar Story

Over 25 Years of Trusted Pharmaceutical Excellence

Sagar Vitaceuticals Nigeria Limited (formerly Strides Vital Nigeria Limited) is a leading name in high-quality pharmaceuticals. Building on a strong foundation that began as a strategic partnership with India’s NSE-listed Strides Arcolabs Ltd, the company transitioned to 100% management ownership in 2017. For over 25 years, our trusted household brands—including Vitaclox, Vitacillin, Vitamox, Tropitone, Inbu, and Ytacan—have provided reliable healthcare solutions across Nigeria.

To meet growing regional demand, we expanded from our initial Ikeja facility to a newly commissioned, state-of-the-art manufacturing plant in Ogun State in August 2023. This modern facility powers our enhanced production capacity across:

  • Tablets & Capsules
  • Liquids & Ointments
  • Dry Powder Sachets

Driven by quality, Focused on Impact

Our Mission

To improve lives by identifying, developing, and commercializing meaningful products that address unmet medical needs. We aim to be the preferred supply partner to all our customers while achieving sustained growth through consistent, customer-centric, and world-class quality products.

Every operational decision at Sagar Vitaceuticals is anchored in three core commitments:

  • Quality first- strict adherence to safety standards across every batch.
  • Value-led operations
  • Empowering work environment for all employees.
